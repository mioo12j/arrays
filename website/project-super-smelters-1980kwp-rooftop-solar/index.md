# Super Smelters: 1980.3 kWp Rooftop Solar Plant

A 1980.3 kWp rooftop solar power plant, about 2 MW, on the open roofs of Super Smelters Ltd., described by the local press as the largest industrial unit in the Jamuria industrial area of West Bengal.

The Project

## About the Super Smelters, West Bengal project

The plant was built in collaboration with Tata Power Solar, with Arrays Ingenieria as implementation partner. It was inaugurated by Super Smelters' director, Sanjay Singhania, with Lt. Gen. Ashish Ranjan Prasad (Retd) of Arrays Ingenieria present.

Super Smelters issued Arrays Ingenieria a letter of appreciation for the project, and the inauguration was covered by Sanmarg and other Hindi dailies.

In 2024 Tata Power Solar engaged Arrays Ingenieria for the installation & commissioning of the 1980 kW sheet-roof plant, the BOS materials and additional cabling, cable trays and earth excavation, the earthing strip, and the supply and service of the module cleaning system.

### Our scope of work

- Installation & commissioning of the 1980 kW sheet-roof plant
- Balance-of-system (BOS) materials and additional cabling
- Cable trays and earth excavation
- Earthing strip and self-drilling fasteners
- Module cleaning system: supply and services
- Testing and commissioning

Installation & Commissioning

## What Our I&C Scope Covered

The standard rooftop installation & commissioning scope we deliver to EPC majors' specifications, after the mandatory quality and safety training and contractor safety sign-off.

### Civil works

- Earth-pit chambers and covers (RCC/PCC)
- Foundations for inverters, ACDB, ITB and accessories
- Cable trenches at ground level

### Mechanical

- Lifting and handling of all material to the roof
- Module mounting structure installation on the sheet roof
- Module installation and interconnection
- Canopies for wall-mounted inverters and junction boxes

### DC side

- String wiring from modules to inverters, tagged and identified
- DC cables in conduit or tray
- MC4 connectors crimped male and female
- Array junction boxes

### AC side and protection

- Inverters and inverter terminal boxes
- AC distribution board (ACDB)
- LT AC cables in trays to the approved layout
- Reverse power relays installed and commissioned

### Earthing and lightning

- Earth electrodes and pits
- Earthing strips for structures, inverters and ACDB, including welding
- Lightning arrestors

### Roof safety and access

- Skylight safety mesh
- Walkways and hand rails
- Safety lifelines for workers
- Scaffolding for installation

### Monitoring, cleaning and handover

- Data monitoring system (SCADA / power manager)
- Module cleaning system with pump and motor
- Safety posters, storage under covers and care until handover
- Testing, commissioning and handover
