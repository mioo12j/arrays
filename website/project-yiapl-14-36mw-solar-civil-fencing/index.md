# Noida International Airport (YIAPL): 14.36 MW Solar, Piling & Fencing

Pile foundations and chain-link perimeter fencing for the 14.36 MW solar power project of YIAPL, the company building Noida International Airport in Uttar Pradesh.

The Project

## About the YIAPL, Uttar Pradesh project

In 2025 Tata Power Renewable Energy engaged Arrays Ingenieria for the construction of pile foundations (300 mm diameter, 1.8 m to 2.3 m deep) and for the supply, civil work and erection of chain-link fencing, with a tentative fence length of about 6.25 km, at the 14.36 MW YIAPL solar power project.

A plant of this size needs its foundations in and its perimeter secured before installation crews can move in.

### Our scope of work

- Pile foundations, 300 mm diameter, 1.8 m to 2.3 m deep
- Supply, civil work and erection of chain-link fencing (about 6.25 km tentative length)
- All manpower, machinery and material for the fencing

## Challenges and How We Handled Them

### An airport site

Work at Noida International Airport runs under strict access and safety control, with every crew and vehicle accounted for.

### Kilometres of fence

Around 6.25 km of chain-link fencing means steady, surveyed progress: posts set in concrete, mesh tensioned, gates aligned.

### Piles to suit the ground

Three pile depths were used, matched to the ground conditions across the 14.36 MW site.
