What We Do

Rooftop Solar

From RCC rooftops to large industrial metal sheds, Ingenieria engineers rooftop systems that maximise generation while protecting your roof's integrity. We manage structural assessment, leak-proof mounting, net-metering liaison and safe commissioning end to end.

Every installation is executed with rigorous safety standards and statutory compliance - the same discipline our clients across India have come to expect and appreciate.

Our Capabilities

  • Structural & shadow analysis
  • Leak-proof ballasted & penetration mounts
  • Net-metering & DISCOM liaison
  • Tier-1 modules & smart inverters
  • Walkways, earthing & safety systems
  • Generation monitoring & reporting

Proven Experience

Our rooftop portfolio includes a 1711.66 kWp on-grid rooftop project in Assam, the 1980.3 kWp Super Smelters industrial plant in Asansol (with Tata Power Solar), rooftop solar at Balaji Action Tesa, Sitarganj, and an RCC rooftop system for Bharat Petroleum in Gurugram.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will rooftop solar damage my roof?
No. We use leak-proof, structurally engineered mounting for both RCC and metal-sheet roofs, backed by load and shadow analysis to protect your roof's integrity.
What is net-metering and do you handle it?
Surplus solar power is exported to the grid and credited against your bill. Ingenieria manages the entire DISCOM net-metering application and approval process for you.
What size rooftop solar system can you install?
From 10 kWp for homes and shops to multi-megawatt industrial rooftops, such as the 1980 kWp Super Smelters and 1711 kWp Assam on-grid plants.
