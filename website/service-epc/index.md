What We Do

## EPC Turnkey

Our turnkey EPC model removes complexity for our clients. Ingenieria owns the entire delivery chain - design engineering, procurement of Tier-1 components, civil and structural works, electrical installation, testing and grid commissioning - backed by a single point of responsibility.

As a trusted implementing partner for **Tata Power Solar**, we have executed EPC scopes to the exacting standards of India's largest solar names, on time and to full client satisfaction.

### Our Capabilities

- Detailed engineering & design
- Tier-1 procurement & logistics
- Civil, structural & electrical execution
- Quality assurance & HSE management
- Testing, commissioning & grid sync
- Documentation & statutory compliance

### Proven Experience

We have delivered turnkey and EPC-scope projects including the **1 MW Jayshree Tea Estate** plant under a Tata Power EPC contract, the **1980.3 kWp Super Smelters** plant with Tata Power Solar, and multiple ground-mount and rooftop plants across India.
