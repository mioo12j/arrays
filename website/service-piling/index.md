# Pile Foundation & Piling

Specialist pile-foundation and piling works - the bedrock of every reliable ground-mount solar plant, executed with precision-engineered rigs and rigorous load testing.

What We Do

## Pile Foundation & Piling

Foundations make or break a solar plant's 25-year life. Ingenieria deploys modern hydraulic piling rigs and proven methodologies - driven, bored and pre-cast pile foundations - engineered to site-specific soil conditions and pull-out load requirements.

Our piling credentials include some of the largest solar sites in the country, delivered safely and on schedule even under demanding ground conditions.

### Our Capabilities

- Geo-technical investigation & pile design
- Driven, bored & pre-cast pile foundations
- Hydraulic rig piling at scale
- Pull-out & load testing
- Pile-cap & pedestal civil works
- Survey, alignment & QA

### Proven Experience

We executed pile-foundation works for the **300 MW SECI project at Koppal, Karnataka** and piling & civil works for the **5.5 MW Tata Motors (TML)** project in Jamshedpur, alongside piling for projects in Bihar and beyond.

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

What solar piling methods do you offer?

Driven, bored and pre-cast pile foundations using modern hydraulic rigs, engineered to site soil conditions and pull-out load requirements.

What is your largest piling project?

Pile-foundation works for the 300 MW SECI solar park at Koppal, Karnataka, and piling and civil works for the 5.5 MW Tata Motors project in Jamshedpur.

Do you carry out pile load testing?

Yes, pull-out and load testing is performed to verify foundation integrity before mounting structures are installed.
