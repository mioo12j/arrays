# SRMB Steel, Durgapur: Rooftop Solar I&C in Three Phases

Three rooftop solar phases on the sheet roofs of SRMB Steel's plant at Durgapur, West Bengal, built by Arrays Ingenieria for Tata Power Solar.

The Project

## About the SRMB Steel, West Bengal project

In 2022 Tata Power Solar placed orders with Arrays Ingenieria for the installation & commissioning of the 1895.4 kWp sheet-roof plant and of the 1060 kWp Phase 2 plant, and for the rooftop BOS services of the 1.69 MWp Phase III.

The scope ran from earth pits and equipment foundations to module mounting structures, cabling, testing and commissioning, followed by the module cleaning service and the cable glands and lugs for each phase in 2023.

### Our scope of work

- Earth pits and equipment foundations (ACDB, inverters, ITB)
- Module mounting structure installation
- Installation & commissioning of the 1895.4 kWp and 1060 kWp plants
- Rooftop BOS services for the 1.69 MWp Phase III
- Module cleaning system service
- Module shifting

On Site

## Challenges and How We Handled Them

### Steel-plant roofs

Steel-plant sheds are high, hot and busy. Lifting material safely to the roof, lifelines and skylight protection came before any module went up.

### Phase after phase

Three phases on one campus over two years meant matching each new phase to the earlier ones, from string layouts to labelling.

### Cleaning built in

Industrial dust makes cleaning essential, so the module cleaning system was installed and commissioned with the plant.

Installation & Commissioning

## What Our I&C Scope Covered

The standard rooftop installation & commissioning scope we deliver to EPC majors' specifications, after the mandatory quality and safety training and contractor safety sign-off.

### Civil works

- Earth-pit chambers and covers (RCC/PCC)
- Foundations for inverters, ACDB, ITB and accessories
- Cable trenches at ground level

### Mechanical

- Lifting and handling of all material to the roof
- Module mounting structure installation on the sheet roof
- Module installation and interconnection
- Canopies for wall-mounted inverters and junction boxes

### DC side

- String wiring from modules to inverters, tagged and identified
- DC cables in conduit or tray
- MC4 connectors crimped male and female
- Array junction boxes

### AC side and protection

- Inverters and inverter terminal boxes
- AC distribution board (ACDB)
- LT AC cables in trays to the approved layout
- Reverse power relays installed and commissioned

### Earthing and lightning

- Earth electrodes and pits
- Earthing strips for structures, inverters and ACDB, including welding
- Lightning arrestors

### Roof safety and access

- Skylight safety mesh
- Walkways and hand rails
- Safety lifelines for workers
- Scaffolding for installation

### Monitoring, cleaning and handover

- Data monitoring system (SCADA / power manager)
- Module cleaning system with pump and motor
- Safety posters, storage under covers and care until handover
- Testing, commissioning and handover
