# Dewan Group of Tea Estates: 1940 kWp Ground-Mount Solar

Three ground-mounted, on-grid solar plants, 1940 kWp in all, for the Dewan Group of Tea Estates of Jay Shree Tea & Industries (BK Birla Group) in Cachar, Assam: 735 kWp at Dewan, 620 kWp at Burtoll and 585 kWp at Labac.

The Project

## About the Jay Shree Tea, Cachar, Assam project

Arrays Ingenieria worked as the implementing partner of Tata Power Renewable Energy Ltd. (TPREL). The work order is dated 25 June 2025; the scope covered supply of the balance of system along with installation & commissioning of the 1940 kWp grid-connected solar power generation system.

Work began on 9 October 2025 and was completed on 8 January 2026, the commissioning date. In its work completion certificate of 22 January 2026, the Dewan Group of Tea Estates confirms that the plants are fully operational, have been taken over, and that performance has been verified and found to meet the agreed standards, to the complete satisfaction of the Dewan Group and TPREL.

Jay Shree Tea also issued a certificate of appreciation for each plant, citing Arrays Ingenieria's professionalism in installation and commissioning: "Their competence is indeed praiseworthy." This was the second Jay Shree Tea solar project for Arrays Ingenieria, after the 1035 kWp at Towkok and Manjushree.

### Our scope of work

- Supply of balance of system (BOS)
- Installation of the ground-mount plants at three estates
- Testing and commissioning of the 1940 kWp grid-connected system
- Handover to the Dewan Group and TPREL

Installation & Commissioning

## What Our I&C Scope Covered

The standard ground-mount installation & commissioning scope we deliver to EPC majors' specifications, after the mandatory quality and safety training and contractor safety sign-off.

### Civil works

- Earth-pit chambers and covers (RCC/PCC)
- Foundations for inverters, ACDB, ITB and accessories
- Cable trenches
- Cube tests to validate concrete strength

### Mechanical

- Module mounting structure installation
- Module installation and interconnection
- Canopies for wall-mounted inverters and junction boxes

### DC side

- String wiring from modules to inverters, tagged and identified
- DC cables in conduit or tray
- MC4 connectors crimped male and female
- Array junction boxes

### AC side and protection

- Inverters and inverter terminal boxes
- AC distribution board (ACDB)
- LT AC cables in trays to the approved layout
- Reverse power relays installed and commissioned

### Earthing and lightning

- Earth electrodes and pits
- Earthing strips for structures, inverters and ACDB, including welding
- Lightning arrestors

### Monitoring and cleaning

- Data monitoring system (SCADA / power manager)
- Module cleaning system with pump and motor
- DWC pipe for RS485 communication cable

### Safety and handover

- Safety lines and safety posters
- Material unloading, storage under covers and care until handover
- Third-party validation of tools where required
- Testing, commissioning and handover
