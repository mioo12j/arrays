What We Do

## Ground-Mount Solar

Ground-mount solar is where Ingenieria's engineering pedigree truly shows. We handle the entire lifecycle - geo-technical survey, optimal tilt and row-spacing design, robust module mounting structures (MMS), DC/AC works and grid synchronisation - delivering plants that generate more, for longer.

Because our teams are drawn entirely from the armed forces, every site runs with military discipline: meticulous planning, strict safety protocols and uncompromising quality control at each milestone.

### Our Capabilities

- Site survey, soil testing & energy-yield modelling
- Hot-dip galvanised module mounting structures
- String & central inverter configurations
- DC/AC cabling, earthing & lightning protection
- Grid synchronisation & commissioning
- Performance testing & handover

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

How much land is needed for a ground-mount solar plant?

Roughly 4 to 5 acres per MW, depending on module efficiency and layout. Ingenieria optimises tilt and row spacing to maximise generation on the available land.

What foundation types do you use for ground-mount solar?

Driven or rammed piles, bored piles, or pre-cast and RCC pedestals, selected from a site-specific geo-technical survey to suit your soil and wind loads.

What ground-mount capacities has Ingenieria delivered?

From sub-megawatt plants to pile foundations for the 300 MW SECI park at Koppal, plus the 10 MW DCM Hisar project and multiple tea-estate plants across Assam.
