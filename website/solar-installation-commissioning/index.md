# Solar Installation & Commissioning (I&C)

Veteran-led site teams that build and commission solar plants for EPC companies, developers and plant owners, to your design and your schedule.

What I&C Covers

## Your design, built and switched on

**Installation & commissioning (I&C)** is the on-site work of turning a solar design and a delivery of equipment into a working, grid-connected plant. It is where most of the risk in a project sits: safety, quality, schedule and the dozens of trades that have to work in sequence.

This is the heart of what Arrays Ingenieria does. Our site teams are led by ex-servicemen who run every job with military discipline, and we work to the specifications, approved makes and quality plans of the EPC or developer we build for.

### Scope we take on

- Pile foundations and module mounting structures
- Module installation
- DC and AC cabling, cable trays and conduits
- Inverters, ACDB/DCDB and LT panels
- Earthing and lightning protection
- Module cleaning systems
- DG synchronisation for sites with diesel generators
- Testing, grid synchronisation and commissioning
- Net-metering and DISCOM approvals when required

### Who we build for

**EPC companies** that need a dependable execution partner: Tata Power has engaged us for piling, civil works and installation from Karnataka to Assam. **Solar developers** running OPEX or RESCO programmes: Sustvest appointed us for the installation and commissioning of 1711.66 kWp in Assam. And **plant owners** who buy their own equipment and want it built properly.

**Modules & electrical**

**Inspection**

**Handover**
