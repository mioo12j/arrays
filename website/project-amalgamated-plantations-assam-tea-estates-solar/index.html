Amalgamated Plantations, Assam: Solar Across Tea Estates

Ground-mount solar plants at the tea estates of Amalgamated Plantations (APPL) across Assam: foundations, balance of system, installation and commissioning, estate by estate.

The Project

About the Amalgamated Plantations, Assam project

From 2022 Tata Power engaged Arrays Ingenieria on the Amalgamated Plantations programme in Assam's tea belt. The first orders covered BOS works for the plantation sites and the shifting of material from the central warehouse to each estate.

In 2023 the company built pile foundations (300 mm diameter, 2.7 m long) for 11 sites totalling 3.8 MWp, and installed and commissioned the ground-mount plants at Chubwa (499 kWp), Kakajan (741 kWp) and Powai (750 kWp).

Phase 2 followed in 2024: installation & commissioning at Nonoi (374 kW) and Diffloo (360 kW), orders for Nahortoli (482 kW), RHRC (122 kW), Achabam (272 kW), Naharkatia (440 kW), Namrup (408 kW) and Borhat (347 kW), and a module cleaning service across the programme.

In 2026 the company carried out BOS works, piling and material transport on the 2862 kWp Amalgamated project for SolarGridX Ventures (Sustvest).

  • Earth pits, equipment foundations and module mounting structures

On Site

Challenges and How We Handled Them

Many estates, one standard

With a dozen estates spread across Upper Assam, the job was as much logistics as engineering: material moved from a central warehouse, crews rotated between gardens, and every site was built and documented the same way.

Soil and monsoon

Tea-garden land is soft and wet for much of the year. Augered piles sized for each site, and equipment raised on proper foundations, keep the plants stable and dry through Assam's monsoon.

Working inside live estates

Plants sit near factories, labour lines and plantation roads. Work was planned around the plucking season and estate traffic, with safety lines and posters on every site.

Installation & Commissioning

What Our I&C Scope Covered

The standard ground-mount installation & commissioning scope we deliver to EPC majors' specifications, after the mandatory quality and safety training and contractor safety sign-off.

Civil works

  • Earth-pit chambers and covers (RCC/PCC)
  • Foundations for inverters, ACDB, ITB and accessories
  • Cable trenches
  • Cube tests to validate concrete strength

Mechanical

  • Module mounting structure installation
  • Module installation and interconnection
  • Canopies for wall-mounted inverters and junction boxes

DC side

  • String wiring from modules to inverters, tagged and identified
  • DC cables in conduit or tray
  • MC4 connectors crimped male and female
  • Array junction boxes

AC side and protection

  • Inverters and inverter terminal boxes
  • AC distribution board (ACDB)
  • LT AC cables in trays to the approved layout
  • Reverse power relays installed and commissioned

Earthing and lightning

  • Earth electrodes and pits
  • Earthing strips for structures, inverters and ACDB, including welding
  • Lightning arrestors

Monitoring and cleaning

  • Data monitoring system (SCADA / power manager)
  • Module cleaning system with pump and motor
  • DWC pipe for RS485 communication cable

Safety and handover

  • Safety lines and safety posters
  • Material unloading, storage under covers and care until handover
  • Third-party validation of tools where required
  • Testing, commissioning and handover
