On Every Site

How We Keep Quality & Safety

01Plan before we build

Method statements, a site safety plan and a programme agreed before work starts.

02Right people, right gear

Trained crews with the personal protective equipment each task needs.

03Checked at every stage

Foundations, structures, cabling and earthing inspected as the work goes up, to the client's or EPC's inspection plan.

04Tested before switch-on

Pre-commissioning tests before a plant is synchronised with the grid.

05Clean, safe sites

Materials stored safely and sites left clean at handover.

06Accountable leadership

A named site lead, with direct access to the company's veteran leadership.
