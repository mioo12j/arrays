O&M & Support

Commissioning is just the beginning. Ingenieria's O&M services keep plants performing through preventive and corrective maintenance, module cleaning, inverter upkeep, performance monitoring and rapid fault resolution - maximising your generation and ROI.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does solar O&M include?
Preventive and corrective maintenance, module cleaning, inverter upkeep, thermography, live performance monitoring and rapid fault resolution.
Can you maintain a plant you didn't build?
Yes. Ingenieria offers O&M and annual maintenance contracts (AMC) for third-party solar plants as well as our own installations.
How do you keep a solar plant at peak performance?
Through live generation monitoring, scheduled servicing, safety checks and statutory compliance — keeping plants at peak yield and always audit-ready.
