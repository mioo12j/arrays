# From Olive Green to Go Green

Arrays Ingenieria is an Udyam-registered MSME founded and led by veterans of the Indian Armed Forces. Building India's solar plants is our second innings of national service.

Who We Are

## A veteran-led MSME, building India's solar plants

Arrays Ingenieria was founded in 2018 by **Lieutenant General Ashish Ranjan Prasad (Retd)**, former Signal Officer-in-Chief of the Indian Army, after nearly four decades of military service. He set it up to give fellow veterans a **second innings**: a new mission, respectable work and a way to keep serving the nation.

The press describes us the same way. Hub Network calls the company "an Ex-Servicemen-led MSME"; The Sentinel writes of "a disciplined, mission-driven transition from OG (Olive Green-the military uniform) to GG (Go Green-renewable energy)".

Under our organisational motto **"Olive Green to Go Green"**, we bring military-grade discipline and engineering precision to deploying critical clean-energy infrastructure. For our team of veterans, working in renewable energy is a **"second innings of national service"**, contributing directly to sustainable nation-building, and to India's [Panchamrit](https://arraysingenieria.com/solar-schemes-india/#panchamrit) climate commitments.

Today we build solar plants across India: installation & commissioning, full EPC and all civil works, on the CAPEX model, for clients from [Tata Power and Jay Shree Tea to Goodricke and Sustvest](https://arraysingenieria.com/clients/).

## How Veterans Run a Solar Site

The habits of service life, applied to every plant we build.

01: **Discipline**

Every site runs to a written programme, reviewed daily, the way an operation is run.

02: **Safety first**

A zero-compromise safety culture, certified to ISO 45001:2018.

03: **Integrity**

Scope, drawings and progress are documented and shared; no surprises at handover.

04: **Mission focus**

Commissioning dates are treated as objectives, not estimates.

05: **Clear command**

One accountable leader for your project, with direct access to the company's leadership.

06: **Teamwork**

Civil, mechanical and electrical crews coordinated as one unit on site.
