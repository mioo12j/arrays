Who We Are

## Where Engineering Meets Resilience

**Ingenieria (Arrays Ingenieria Pvt. Ltd.)** is a renewable-energy EPC company founded in 2018 and run entirely by former military personnel. The name "Ingeniería" means engineering in Spanish, a fitting symbol of our commitment to precision, innovation and discipline in everything we build.

We design, engineer and deliver **ground-mount and rooftop solar power plants** across India, along with pile foundations, civil works, chain-link fencing, grid commissioning and long-term operations & maintenance. From a single rooftop to a 300 MW utility-scale solar park, we approach every project with the same steadfast focus on excellence, safety and sustainability.

Our ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certifications reflect an unwavering dedication to quality, environmental stewardship and occupational safety, and our partnerships with industry leaders such as **Tata Power Solar, Tata Steel, Tata Motors, DCM, Super Smelters and Jay Shree Tea** have established us as a trusted, emerging leader in renewable energy.
