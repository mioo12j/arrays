Why Go Solar

The Smartest Investment a Business or Home Can Make

Electricity is one of the largest recurring costs for Indian industries, institutions and households — and grid tariffs only climb year after year. A well-engineered solar power plant turns that liability into a 25-year asset: it slashes your monthly bills from day one, protects you from tariff hikes, and typically pays for itself in just 3 to 5 years. Everything after that is effectively free, clean power.

With falling module prices, accelerated depreciation for businesses, and the government's PM Surya Ghar subsidy for residential rooftops, there has never been a better time to switch. FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about going solar with Ingenieria.

What does Ingenieria (Arrays Ingenieria Pvt. Ltd.) do?
We are a veteran-led, ISO 9001/14001/45001-certified solar EPC company operating Pan-India. We design and build ground-mount and rooftop solar power plants and handle pile foundations, civil works, chain-link fencing, grid commissioning and long-term operations & maintenance — from a single rooftop to 300 MW utility-scale parks.
How much does a solar power system cost in India?
As a guide, installed cost ranges from about ₹42,000–₹60,000 per kWp depending on whether it is utility ground-mount, industrial, commercial or residential rooftop. Use our free Solar Savings Calculator for a personalised estimate including subsidy and payback.
Is there a government subsidy for solar?
Yes. Under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, residential rooftop systems are eligible for a central subsidy of up to ₹78,000 (₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW + ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW). Businesses can also claim accelerated depreciation. Our calculator factors the residential subsidy in automatically.
What is the payback period and how much can I save?
Most of our clients see a payback of 3–5 years, after which the system keeps generating largely free power for its 25+ year life — often delivering several times the investment in lifetime savings, plus a major cut in carbon emissions.
Should I choose ground-mount or rooftop solar?
Rooftop solar is ideal when you have unused roof space and want to offset on-site consumption. Ground-mount solar suits larger capacities and open land, delivering maximum generation. We assess your load, space and goals during a free site survey and recommend the optimal mix.
Do you handle approvals, net-metering and subsidy paperwork?
Yes — we manage the complete process: structural & shadow analysis, DISCOM liaison, net-metering applications, subsidy documentation, grid synchronisation and safety-compliant commissioning. It's true single-window, turnkey delivery.
Which areas of India do you serve?
We work Pan-India — with completed and ongoing projects across Haryana, Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha, Assam and Karnataka — operated from our Greater Noida (UP) corporate office and Madhubani (Bihar) branch.
Why choose a veteran-led solar company?
Ingenieria is founded and run entirely by former military professionals. That means discipline, meticulous planning, strict safety, and uncompromising on-time delivery on every site — the same values that have earned us repeat trust from Tata Power Solar, Tata Steel, Tata Motors, Super Smelters and Jay Shree Tea.
