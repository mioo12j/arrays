# Inauguration of the 595 kWp Koomber Solar Plant

1 Oct 2026 · Koomber Tea Estate, Cachar, Assam

The inauguration ceremony at the Koomber Tea Estate 595 kWp solar plant, 1 October 2026, Arrays Ingenieria installation partner. On 1 October 2026 the Chief Minister of Assam, Dr Himanta Biswa Sarma, inaugurated the 595 kWp ground-mounted, on-grid solar power plant at Koomber Tea Estate in Cachar, Assam. Part of the 3.11 MW Goodricke Tea Estates Solar Programme developed by Tata Power Renewable Energy and Sustvest. Arrays Ingenieria (Ingenieria) is an ex-servicemen-led solar EPC and installation MSME.

Filmed at the plant on 1 October 2026. Length: 12 seconds, no sound needed.
