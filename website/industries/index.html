For homes & businesses

Whether you want to cut a soaring household bill or take an entire factory off the grid in daytime, we right-size the system to your load, roof or land, and budget. Residential clients benefit from the PM Surya Ghar subsidy; commercial and industrial clients gain from accelerated depreciation and rapid 3–5 year paybacks.

For institutions & government

We deliver to the documentation, safety and compliance standards public projects demand, from schools and hospitals to large PSU and utility-scale parks such as the 300 MW SECI project at Koppal. Our ex-servicemen team is built for exactly this kind of disciplined, accountable execution.
