What We Do

Civil Works & Fencing

A solar plant is only as secure as its perimeter and as durable as its civil works. Ingenieria delivers supply, civil work and erection of chain-link fencing, pre-cast and cast-in-situ boundary walls, foundations, drains and roads - engineered for longevity and security.

Our civil teams bring the same discipline and attention to detail that defines our solar EPC work, ensuring every site is safe, compliant and built to last.

Our Capabilities

  • Supply & erection of chain-link fencing
  • Pre-cast & RCC boundary walls
  • Foundations, pedestals & pile caps
  • Internal roads, drains & civil BOS
  • Site grading & earthworks
  • Security infrastructure

Proven Experience

Our civil scope includes supply, civil work and erection of chain-link fencing for the 14.36 MW YIAPL Solar Power Project in Uttar Pradesh, a pre-cast boundary wall for Tata Motors, Jamshedpur, and chain-link fencing & civil works in Madhepura, Bihar.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What civil works do you provide for solar projects?
Pre-cast and RCC boundary walls, chain-link fencing, foundations and pedestals, internal roads, drains and site grading, the full civil balance-of-system.
Can you secure and fence a large solar site?
Yes. For example, we delivered supply, civil work and erection of chain-link fencing for the 14.36 MW YIAPL solar power project in Uttar Pradesh.
Do you take standalone civil contracts?
Yes, including pre-cast boundary walls such as the one delivered for Tata Motors, Jamshedpur, alongside full solar EPC scopes.
