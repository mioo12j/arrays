Tata Motors, Jamshedpur: Piling, Civil Works and I&C for 5.5 MW Solar

Piling and civil works for the 5.5 MW solar plant at Tata Motors in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand.

Home/Projects/Tata Motors, Jamshedpur · 5.5 MW
The Project

About the Tata Motors, Jamshedpur project

Tata Power issued Arrays Ingenieria the work order on 27 July 2023 for piling and civil work for the 5.5 MW plant at Tata Motors, Jamshedpur. The scope included a pre-cast boundary wall around the plant.

Further orders followed through 2023 and 2024: land development, pull-out tests on the piles, gate fabrication, a temporary water supply, liaison with government offices and, from December 2023, the installation & commissioning of the 5.579 MWp plant at Jojobera Yard together with its module cleaning system.

Our scope of work

  • Pile foundations (300 mm dia, 1.8 m deep) and pull-out tests
  • Land development and a pre-cast boundary wall
  • Installation & commissioning of the 5.579 MWp plant at Jojobera Yard
  • Module cleaning system
  • Gate fabrication, temporary water supply and statutory liaison

Installation & Commissioning

What Our I&C Scope Covered

The standard ground-mount installation & commissioning scope we deliver to EPC majors' specifications, after the mandatory quality and safety training and contractor safety sign-off.

Civil works

  • Earth-pit chambers and covers (RCC/PCC)
  • Foundations for inverters, ACDB, ITB and accessories
  • Cable trenches
  • Cube tests to validate concrete strength

Mechanical

  • Module mounting structure installation
  • Module installation and interconnection
  • Canopies for wall-mounted inverters and junction boxes

DC side

  • String wiring from modules to inverters, tagged and identified
  • DC cables in conduit or tray
  • MC4 connectors crimped male and female
  • Array junction boxes

AC side and protection

  • Inverters and inverter terminal boxes
  • AC distribution board (ACDB)
  • LT AC cables in trays to the approved layout
  • Reverse power relays installed and commissioned

Earthing and lightning

  • Earth electrodes and pits
  • Earthing strips for structures, inverters and ACDB, including welding
  • Lightning arrestors

Monitoring and cleaning

  • Data monitoring system (SCADA / power manager)
  • Module cleaning system with pump and motor
  • DWC pipe for RS485 communication cable

Safety and handover

  • Safety lines and safety posters
  • Material unloading, storage under covers and care until handover
  • Third-party validation of tools where required
  • Testing, commissioning and handover
