# Rossell India Tea Estates, Assam: Ground-Mount Solar I&C

Four ground-mount solar plants for Rossell India's tea estates in Assam, each built from the foundations up to commissioning by Arrays Ingenieria for Tata Power.

The Project

## About the Rossell India, Assam project

Kharikatia Tea Estate (302 kW, 2023) came first: pile construction, installation & commissioning of the ground-mount plant and its BOS works.

At Nagrijuli Tea Estate the company piled the site in 2024 and installed and commissioned the 493.24 kWp plant in 2025, with its BOS materials, additional cabling and module cleaning system. Romai Tea Estate (170.51 kWp) followed the same pattern in 2025, and in 2026 the company piled, supplied BOS materials for, and installed and commissioned the 367 kW plant at Dikom Tea Estate.

### Our scope of work

- Pile foundations
- Earth pits, equipment foundations and module mounting structures
- Installation & commissioning of each ground-mount plant
- BOS materials, inverter stands and additional cabling
- Module cleaning system services

On Site

## Challenges and How We Handled Them

### Four estates, four sizes

From 170 kWp at Romai to 493 kWp at Nagrijuli, each plant was sized to the estate's load and land, but built to the same EPC specification and checklists.

### Repeat client, rising scale

Rossell's estates came back for a second, third and fourth plant between 2023 and 2026, each time with the full chain of piling, BOS, I&C and cleaning.

### Foundations first

Piling was done ahead of installation on every estate, so structures went up level and true on soft tea-garden soil.

Installation & Commissioning

## What Our I&C Scope Covered

The standard ground-mount installation & commissioning scope we deliver to EPC majors' specifications, after the mandatory quality and safety training and contractor safety sign-off.

### Civil works

- Earth-pit chambers and covers (RCC/PCC)
- Foundations for inverters, ACDB, ITB and accessories
- Cable trenches
- Cube tests to validate concrete strength

### Mechanical

- Module mounting structure installation
- Module installation and interconnection
- Canopies for wall-mounted inverters and junction boxes

### DC side

- String wiring from modules to inverters, tagged and identified
- DC cables in conduit or tray
- MC4 connectors crimped male and female
- Array junction boxes

### AC side and protection

- Inverters and inverter terminal boxes
- AC distribution board (ACDB)
- LT AC cables in trays to the approved layout
- Reverse power relays installed and commissioned

### Earthing and lightning

- Earth electrodes and pits
- Earthing strips for structures, inverters and ACDB, including welding
- Lightning arrestors

### Monitoring and cleaning

- Data monitoring system (SCADA / power manager)
- Module cleaning system with pump and motor
- DWC pipe for RS485 communication cable

### Safety and handover

- Safety lines and safety posters
- Material unloading, storage under covers and care until handover
- Third-party validation of tools where required
- Testing, commissioning and handover
