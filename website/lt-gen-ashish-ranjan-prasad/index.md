Lt. Gen. Ashish Ranjan Prasad (Retd), founder and CEO of Arrays Ingenieria.

**Key facts**

- Joined the Indian Army in January 1981, in the Corps of Signals; studied at BIT Mesra, Ranchi.
- Commanded 23 Infantry Division at Ranchi as a Major General, and was promoted Lieutenant General on 1 August 2016.
- Served as Signal Officer-in-Chief and Senior Colonel Commandant of the Corps of Signals until his retirement on 31 July 2018.
- Awarded the Vishisht Seva Medal (Republic Day 2016) and the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (Republic Day 2017); ADC and Ph.D.
- Founded Arrays Ingenieria in 2018 under the motto "Olive Green to Go Green" and leads it as Chief Executive Officer.

## At a glance

Lieutenant General Ashish Ranjan Prasad (Retd), AVSM, VSM, ADC, Ph.D, is a retired three-star officer of the Indian Army and the founder and Chief Executive Officer of Arrays Ingenieria Pvt. Ltd. (INGENIERIA), an ex-servicemen-led solar EPC and installation & commissioning company. He spent his military career in the Corps of Signals, the arm that carries the Army's communications, and retired as its head, the Signal Officer-in-Chief. Colleagues, clients and the press usually refer to him as **Lt. Gen. A.R. Prasad (Retd)**.

## Education and early career

Lt. Gen. Prasad studied at the Birla Institute of Technology (BIT), Mesra, in Ranchi, one of eastern India's best-known engineering institutions ([Jharkhand State News, 1 August 2016](https://jharkhandstatenews.com/article/top-stories/964/major-general-ashish-ranjan-prasad-becomes-lt-general)). That technical grounding shaped the rest of his career: he chose the Corps of Signals, the Army's engineering and communications arm.

He joined the Indian Army in January 1981, in the Corps of Signals ([Jharkhand State News](https://jharkhandstatenews.com/article/top-stories/964/major-general-ashish-ranjan-prasad-becomes-lt-general)). Over the decades that followed he rose through the ranks of the Corps to general rank.

**What the Corps of Signals does**

The Corps of Signals provides the Indian Army's communications: radio, line, satellite and data networks that link every headquarters and unit, and the electronic-warfare and information-technology systems that sit on them. Its officers are engineers and commanders at the same time.

## Commanding 23 Infantry Division, Ranchi

As a Major General, Lt. Gen. Prasad commanded the Indian Army's 23 Infantry Division at Ranchi, Jharkhand ([Jharkhand State News](https://jharkhandstatenews.com/article/top-stories/964/major-general-ashish-ranjan-prasad-becomes-lt-general)). Command of an infantry division, a formation of several brigades and thousands of troops, is a demanding field command, responsible for the operational readiness, training and welfare of the soldiers under it.

On Republic Day 2016, while still a Major General, he was awarded the **Vishisht Seva Medal (VSM)** for distinguished service; he is listed at serial 24 of the VSM awards as "Maj Gen Ashish Ranjan Prasad, Signals" ([Republic Day 2016 honours list](http://indianmilitaryveterans.blogspot.com/2016/01/list-of-personnel-being-awarded.html)).

## Promotion to Lieutenant General

On 1 August 2016 he was promoted to the rank of Lieutenant General, the Army's three-star rank. The then Chief of the Army Staff, General Dalbir Singh Suhag, handed over the promoted rank to him ([Jharkhand State News, 1 August 2016](https://jharkhandstatenews.com/article/top-stories/964/major-general-ashish-ranjan-prasad-becomes-lt-general)). By then he had completed 35 years of service.

## Signal Officer-in-Chief of the Indian Army

As a Lieutenant General he was appointed **Signal Officer-in-Chief** (SO-in-C) and **Senior Colonel Commandant** of the Corps of Signals. The Signal Officer-in-Chief heads the Corps at Army Headquarters in New Delhi and is responsible for the communication and information-technology backbone of the whole Army; the Senior Colonel Commandant is the senior-most officer of the Corps and the guardian of its traditions.

### Building a digital Army

In March 2017 he addressed DEFCOM INDIA 2017, a two-day seminar organised by the Corps of Signals with the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) on the theme "Infrastructure and Skilled Human Resource for Digital Army". The Press Information Bureau reported that the event was addressed by "Signal Officer-in-Chief and Senior Col. Commandant, Corps of Signals Lt. Gen. Ashish Ranjan Prasad" alongside the Director General of CII, with the Chief of the Army Staff, General Bipin Rawat, and the Minister of State for Defence, Dr. Subhash Bhamre, also speaking ([PIB via IndraStra, 24 March 2017](https://www.indrastra.com/2017/03/NEWS-Increasing-Infrastructure-for-Digital-Army-003-03-2017-0079.html)). At the same event he laid out the outline of **smart battlefields**, networked and sensor-rich, for the Army of the future ([National Defence Bureau video, 23 March 2017](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-8CUv9HKaI0)).

Lt. Gen. A.R. Prasad as Signal Officer-in-Chief, delivering the keynote at DEFCOM India.

### Joint communications and need-based networks

When he retired, the news agency IANS reported that he was known for his efforts towards integrating the communications of the three services, the Army, Navy and Air Force, and that he introduced need-based networks built on the internet and commercial networks, protected by multiple layers of security ([IANS via Business Standard, 31 July 2018](https://www.business-standard.com/article/news-ians/army-s-signals-chief-retires-118073101468_1.html)). Both ideas, jointness and secure use of commercial technology, remain central to India's military modernisation.

### On the boards of two public-sector companies

During his tenure he also served as a director on the boards of two central public-sector enterprises: **Electronics Corporation of India Ltd. (ECIL)** and, as a nominee director, **ITI Ltd.**, the state-owned telecom manufacturer. Company records show both directorships ending on 31 July 2018, the day he retired ([ECIL](https://www.zaubacorp.com/ELECTRONICS-CORPORATION-OF-INDIA-LIMITED-U32100TG1967GOI001149); [ITI Ltd.](https://www.zaubacorp.com/ITI-LIMITED-L32202KA1950GOI000640), MCA records via ZaubaCorp).

## Retirement after 37 years of service

Lt. Gen. Prasad retired as Signal Officer-in-Chief on 31 July 2018 ([IANS, 31 July 2018](https://www.business-standard.com/article/news-ians/army-s-signals-chief-retires-118073101468_1.html)), after more than 37 years in uniform that began as a young Signals officer in 1981 and ended at the head of his Corps.

## Second innings: founding Arrays Ingenieria

In 2018 he founded [Arrays Ingenieria Pvt. Ltd.](https://arraysingenieria.com/about/), known as INGENIERIA, to give fellow veterans a second innings of national service in clean energy. The company's motto, **"Olive Green to Go Green"**, captures the move from the Army's olive-green uniform to renewable energy. Assam's daily The Sentinel described the company as "a unique MSME founded by ex-servicemen, symbolizing a disciplined, mission-driven transition from OG (Olive Green-the military uniform) to GG (Go Green-renewable energy)" ([The Sentinel, 22 May 2025](https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/assam-news/assam-aipl-commissions-1-mw-solar-power-plant-at-jayshree-tea-estate)).

Under his leadership the company works pan India as a [CAPEX solar EPC](https://arraysingenieria.com/capex-solar-epc/) contractor and as an [installation & commissioning partner](https://arraysingenieria.com/solar-installation-commissioning/) to India's largest solar developers, with civil works, pile foundations and operations & maintenance alongside. It holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification, and its site teams are drawn largely from the armed forces. Read more in [our ex-servicemen story](https://arraysingenieria.com/ex-servicemen-led-msme/).

As a team comprised entirely of former military professionals, we bring a distinct approach to every challenge, strategic planning, decisive action, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.
